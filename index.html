🔬 Grammar lab · Past continuous with past simple

Past continuous (a longer action)

I / he / she / it → was + verb-ing

you / we / they → were + verb-ing

Negative: wasn't / weren't + verb-ing

Past simple (a short action)

cook → cooked · ring → rang

see → saw · catch → caught

It interrupts the longer action!

Look!
past continuous + when + past simple
past simple + while + past continuous
